# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Navet's board operates recruitment in Bifrost. Applicants use an existing ifinavet.no account.
## Product Purpose
Collect applications, coordinate two-person interviews, support board-led selection and communicate decisions.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Admissions are independent of semester records. Configurable application and interview windows up to two weeks. Default interviews last 15 minutes with a 5-minute buffer. Availability, Google calendar conflicts, rooms and breaks constrain scheduling. The board approves schedules and separately sends decisions. Application data, notes and admission history are deleted when the admission closes; ordinary member accounts survive.
The applicant form is available only during an active application window. Otherwise show that applications are closed and next open at the start of the following spring/autumn semester. Enforce the same window on server-side submission.
## Product Principles
Reuse existing identity, onboarding and delivery logic. Human selection decisions. Minimal Norwegian copy. No decorative text separators. Calendar and candidate comparison are the primary views.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing Bifrost sidebar, UI components and tokens. Preview candidates are synthetic and local-only.
